## Contradictions with our assumptions
- "Subqueries not allowed in alerts" is only half right: FROM-subquery nested aggregation is documented as allowed (ALR:482-540) with the restrictions above. The WHERE/SELECT subqueries and JOIN are not.
- "clamp_min has no form" is wrong for widgets: clamp_min/clamp_max are NRQL math functions (nrql-math-using-select.mdx:51).
- RLIKE/NOT RLIKE and LIKE wildcards are documented; the model gap is ours.
- Explicit SINCE in a widget does not override the time picker by default, but a per-widget "Ignore time picker" toggle is documented.
